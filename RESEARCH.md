# Revieve: product and market notes

Independent research from public sources, October 2026. Not affiliated with Revieve.

## The business

Revieve Oy (founded 2016, Helsinki, with a Chicago office and teams in Valencia and Seoul) sells an enterprise SaaS platform of AI advisors for beauty brands and retailers: selfie-based skin analysis, AR makeup try-on, foundation matching, and product recommendation modules for skincare, makeup, haircare, suncare, nutrition and fragrance. Buyers are beauty brands (Unilever, Oriflame, BABOR, Paula's Choice) and retailers (Boots, CVS, JCPenney, Debenhams, AS Watson, Matas Group). Pricing is not public; deals start with a demo and deployment is white-labeled into the partner's web, app, in-store and social channels. The company says it works with 120+ brands and retailers in 40+ countries and reaches millions of end users. Disclosed funding is small: about EUR 570k seed (2017) and EUR 1.3M Series A (2019) led by Virala via Valeado AB with Business Finland and angel Leena Niemisto, roughly USD 2M total in public databases, with PitchBook listing two further undisclosed rounds into 2021 (USD 3.11M raised to date). A 2026 job post states the company is profitable, with 30+ staff and about a dozen engineers.

## Market

Selfie-based diagnostics and AR try-on have moved from novelty to standard merchandising tooling at large beauty retailers. In-store adoption is scaling: Haut.AI expanded with Grupo Boticario from a 24-store pilot to nearly 4,000 Brazilian stores in 2026. Discovery is shifting into conversational surfaces, which is why Revieve shipped an AI Skin Advisor for ChatGPT (March 2026) and a WhatsApp skin advisor with ERHA in Indonesia (July 2026). Selfies are biometric data, so GDPR and the EU AI Act make privacy posture a purchasing criterion for European retailers.

## Product notes

- The advisor suite covers more categories than most rivals (nutrition, suncare, fragrance, hair color) and ships across web, app, in-store screens and now ChatGPT and WhatsApp.
- The Matas Group deployment (May 2026) reports conversion uplifts of +331% for virtual try-on, +180% for skin analytics and +153% for the makeup advisor, with AOV up 14 to 37%.
- September 2026 brought BeautyCatalyst, a commerce intelligence layer meant to connect diagnostics data with product and market intelligence, positioned for "the agentic AI era".
- Friction likely sits in integration effort per partner and in proving diagnostic consistency across skin tones, lighting and devices, where Haut.AI competes on published rigor.
- No public pricing or self-serve tier, versus Perfect Corp's free-trial plugins and Haut.AI's usage-based API.

## Opportunities

- An advisor operations console: partner teams need session funnels, failure reasons (bad selfie, timeout, low confidence) and latency percentiles in one view, which fits BeautyCatalyst's pitch.
- Eval harness for recommendation quality across skin tones and lighting, answering the fairness claims rivals publish.
- Conversational surface coverage beyond ChatGPT and WhatsApp (retail chat, in-store kiosk assistants) with the same advisor backend.
- Self-serve onboarding for indie brands, a segment the site targets but that enterprise sales motions serve poorly.
